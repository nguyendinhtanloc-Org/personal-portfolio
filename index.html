01

Who I am, in a few honest lines

I'm a final-year Data Engineering student (class of 2026) working across pipelines, analytics, and computer vision. The overlap is the point: a pipeline that can't be verified is a liability, and a model nobody can reproduce is a demo.

How I work. I keep design and evidence separate. If I say a system is at-least-once or doesn't lose data, that comes with the test that shows it — reconciliation queries, kill/restart runs, measured latencies. Anything unproven is labeled an estimate, never a result.

Where I point my curiosity next: distributed systems internals (delivery semantics, backpressure, replay), warehouse design at scale, and vision models that ship, not just benchmark.

marginalia. The rule above is the difference between a portfolio and a design doc: claims I can't demonstrate are marked estimate, never presented as results.
